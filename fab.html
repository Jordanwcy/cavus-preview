Design the fibre. Then make it.

Choose a nested anti-resonant geometry and CavusFab computes where it transmits, how much light it loses, how tightly it bends, the preform you would stack to draw it, and the draw parameters to start from. Then an optimizer searches the design space for a better fibre than the one you drew.

Preform recipe — what to stack, what to draw

Two draw stages sized from real preform dimensions: 25 mm stack → 3 mm cane → fibre OD 200 µm (×8.3 then ×15, aspect-preserving). Draw starting points from the Cavus Fabrication Playbook (Jasion 2019 · Southampton 2024 · Nature Photonics 2025). Junction — connect this hollow-core fibre to the installed solid-fibre world

Junction model = CavusSim v0 (Marcuse Gaussian overlap + Fresnel + 0.10 dB hollow-core mode-shape term), validated against the 1–3 dB naive-butt and 0.15–0.5 dB adapted-junction anchors. The hollow-core mode-field diameter is derived from this design as MFD ≈ 1.36 × R (fits the DNANF literature estimate of 20 µm at R = 14.75 µm); a vector mode solver replaces this in v1.
