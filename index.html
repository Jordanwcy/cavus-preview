ATTENUATION RECORD
2019 – 2026

The record is falling. Fast.

For forty years, solid glass had a floor nothing could beat. In 2025, hollow-core fibre went under it — and kept going. This is what a technology looks like right before it matters.

SECTION02
WHY NOW
THE CLOSING WINDOW

The window is open. Briefly.

Hollow-core fibre just left the laboratory. The layer we work on — connecting it to the world — is still unclaimed.

2022

Microsoft acquires Lumenisity, the Southampton hollow-core spin-out. Hyperscalers begin buying the supply chain, not just the fibre.

2025

Hollow beats solid for the first time — 0.091 dB/km in Nature Photonics, below silica's forty-year floor. Over 1,200 km already carrying live Azure traffic.

2026

The record falls again in China — 0.04 dB/km — and commercial hollow-core links now run between Guangdong and Hong Kong. The Greater Bay Area is a live deployment zone.

Now

Interconnect, cabling and qualification remain open. The standards are unwritten and the toll booth is unbuilt. We estimate 18–24 months to take a seat at that table.

SECTION04
ONE LINK
THREE PROBLEMS

We work where deployments succeed or fail.

Every hollow-core deployment must join the world's installed glass on one side and an AI cluster on the other. That link is our laboratory.

01

Join hollow to solid

Mode-field adapters, splicing protocols, field-installable connectors between air-core and the installed base — the toll booth every deployment pays.

02

Make it deployable

From lab spools to data-centre-ready cable: bend-tolerant designs, standards qualification, pilot manufacturing with industrial partners.

03

Prove where it pays

Measure where lower latency actually earns its keep in GPU clusters and inter-DC links — published, then deployed, Greater Bay Area first.
